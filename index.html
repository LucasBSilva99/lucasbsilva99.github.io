About

Software and machine learning, applied to real data.

I'm a software developer with a Master's in Computer Science from FCUL and a focus on artificial intelligence. My thesis used deep learning to predict how ALS progresses, and I've kept building in that direction since: convolutional networks, genetic algorithms, agent simulations and parallel feature extraction.

Since April 2024 I've been a Python & AI Developer at Siemens, where I build AI tools like a payment-to-invoice matching system, develop a pension investment insights application, and am at the forefront of my department's AI initiatives as one of its key experts and main point of contact on AI. Before that I was a Data Engineer at KPMG in Lisbon, building ETL and analytics workflows with Python, SQL and Alteryx.
